# Data Retention

Version: 2026-10-01

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This document says how long Hiyall keeps each kind of information. A cleanup routine runs every hour on our servers and removes what has expired, in small batches. Items in square brackets are targets or decisions that the operator or counsel must confirm before this is published, because the period is not yet fixed.

## Your account

- **Download my data:** Settings > Account > Download my data. It includes your profile and activity, your support conversations and your billing records (coin balance and history, purchases and subscriptions, without the store's identifiers), if you have any.
- **Delete my account:** Settings > Account > Delete account (or ask us: a staff member can delete the account on request with an audited tool). [CONFIRM: grace period] Your account is hidden and signed out straight away. If you change your mind, you have 30 days to cancel. After that your data is erased on a schedule, apart from the exceptions below. If support schedules the deletion of an account that is suspended or banned, the member cannot cancel it.

## After you delete your account

- **Content** such as posts, comments, stories and photos is removed. Copies others saved are theirs.
- **Messages** you sent and received remain on the other person's phone.
- **Support conversations and billing records** (coins, purchases and subscriptions) are deleted with your account. The stores that took a payment keep their own records. A subscription bought from Google Play or Apple is not cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in the store first.
- **Reports** about you, or made by you, and any evidence held for them, may be kept for the periods above.
- **Chat photos** held for a report stay until counsel sets a release rule [COUNSEL: chat evidence release rule], even though your account is erased. [COUNSEL: scanning status at launch. Keep only if photos are scanned at launch. Wording if live: Flagged photos held as evidence, and their case records, stay for the periods above even though your account is erased.]
- **Third parties:** your sign-in record is removed from Firebase Authentication. If you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to end the link between Hiyall and your Apple account when the app could get Apple's code; otherwise you remove it in your Apple ID settings. [COUNSEL: confirm this satisfies Apple's rules and any law on ending the link when an account is deleted.]
- **Backups** may still hold your data until they expire, as described in the table.
- **Anything we must keep by law**, or need to keep to protect someone from serious harm, is kept only for as long as that reason applies, with limited access.
