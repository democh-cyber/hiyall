# Community Guidelines

Version: 2026-10-01

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Hiyall is a place to find your people. These guidelines keep it a place people want to be. They apply everywhere in the app: profiles, posts, stories, comments, spaces and chats.

## The basics

- **Be kind.** Disagree without attacking.
- **Be real.** Use your real age. Do not pretend to be another person.
- **Respect "no".** If someone does not want to talk, stop. Blocking is always their right.
- **Look out for each other.** If something feels wrong, report it.

## What is not allowed

**Harassment and bullying.** No insults aimed at a person, pile-ons, stalking, repeated unwanted messages, or contacting someone who has blocked you (including from another account).

**Hate.** No attacks on people for who they are, such as their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.

**Threats and violence.** No threats, no encouraging violence, no showing violence to shock or celebrate it.

**Sexual content.** No nudity or sexual content in profiles, photos, posts, stories or spaces. Never send sexual messages or images to anyone who has not clearly asked for them. **Never send sexual content to, or about, anyone under 18. Never ask a minor for sexual content or for a meeting. This is zero tolerance, and we will remove the account and, where the law requires, report it.**

**Child safety.** Adults and teens are kept apart on Hiyall. Do not try to get around that, for example by lying about your age or by contacting teens somewhere else after meeting them here.

**Self-harm.** Do not encourage or glorify self-harm, eating disorders or suicide. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a local helpline. If someone says they are in danger, contact local emergency services, and report the post so we can look.

**Scams and spam.** No fake giveaways, requests for money, phishing, mass messaging, fake accounts, or selling things to members who did not ask.

**Impersonation.** No pretending to be someone else, a public figure, or Hiyall staff.

**Private information.** Do not share other people's private information, such as phone numbers, addresses, school, passwords or private photos, without their permission. Do not share someone's private chat to embarrass them.

**Illegal activity.** No selling drugs, weapons or stolen goods, no illegal services, and nothing else that breaks the law.

**Cheating the system.** No fake accounts to avoid a block or a ban, no tools that collect people's profiles in bulk, and no attempts to find where someone is by faking your location.

## Meeting people safely

- Do not share your address, school, or daily routine with someone you only know online.
- Nearby shows a rough distance. Turn off location for Hiyall in your phone's settings if you would rather not use it.
- If you decide to meet someone, tell a friend or family member, meet in a public place, and think about going with a friend. Teens should always tell a trusted adult.
- Anyone can say anything about themselves. A profile or an age on Hiyall may not be true.
- Chats are private and we cannot read them. That means blocking and reporting matter more.

## Your controls

- **Block.** They cannot find, see or message you, and you cannot see them. We do not tell them.
- **Mute.** Their posts and activity stop showing up for you.
- **Restrict.** Their chats leave your unread count, and read receipts and typing are off. Their comments on your posts are visible only to them.
- **Report.** From a profile, a post, a chat or a single message.
- **Online and last seen.** Settings has a switch to hide when you are online.

## Reporting

You can report a person, a chat, a message, a post, a comment or a story. Choose the reason that fits best. Reports about serious harm are looked at first.

- For chats, you decide which messages (up to 10) and photos to include. Chats are end-to-end encrypted, so we can only see what you send us.
- The person you report is not told it was you.
- You can also block the person in the same step.
- You can see the status of your reports in the app.

## What happens next

A person on our team looks at the report. Depending on what we find, we may:

- dismiss it, if nothing breaks the rules;
- warn the person;
- remove content;
- suspend the account. A suspended account is signed out straight away.
