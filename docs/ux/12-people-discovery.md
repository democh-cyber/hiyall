# People discovery and temporary filters

The Find People interface uses a full-screen filter surface with a compact header, a removable active-filter summary, interest chips, a single vibe, searchable languages, and progressively disclosed country selection. Lime, lavender, peach, and mint distinguish dimensions without neon effects. Dark and warm light themes share the same layout. Content scrolls above the separate bottom actions, with safe-area space and at least 48 logical-pixel controls. Reduced motion skips size animation entirely.

Filters form a temporary draft. Closing or going back discards changes. Clear all resets only that draft. Find people applies it to the current search; there is no saved-preference write or silent persistence. Genuine shortcuts can copy the owner's actual interests, language codes, or current vibe; Global clears only country. Language names come from the existing ISO label inventory, with duplicate name aliases removed from the picker. Search sends codes, never labels or language proficiency as a search field.

## Implemented service

`POST /v1/discovery/search` consumes optional `interests`, `languages`, `country`, `vibe`, and `cursor`. Collections accept at most 10 distinct values. Interest and vibe comparisons ignore case; language tags normalize case, country uses uppercase codes. Within a collection, at least one selected value must match; different dimensions combine with AND. Vibe matches the existing free-text current-vibe field exactly apart from case. The chips are suggestions, not a new profile enum or personality score.

Search requires authentication and a completed onboarding; there is no eligibility step, so every onboarded member can search. Every candidate must be active, have finished onboarding, be discoverable and public (the defaults for every member, see [Nearby and open access](../api/19-nearby-and-open-access.md)), be unblocked in both directions and be in the searcher's age band (minors and adults do not see each other). A filtered field must explicitly have the `everyone` audience; matching private fields is forbidden even if the response would omit them. This prevents result-based inference of hidden interests, languages, vibe, or country. Every page repeats these checks against the authoritative database snapshot.

Responses contain only an allowlist: app user ID for profile routing, handle, name, headline, bio, and permitted interests, languages, vibe, and country. No email, phone, exact location, birth date, internal provider UID, account balance, session information, last-seen time, private counts, or age-assurance metadata is returned. An `online` flag appears (as `true`) only for a member who lets everyone see presence and is connected right now; otherwise it is absent. Tapping a person opens the existing authorized profile route, which rechecks current access. Returning to results refreshes the search so changed blocks and visibility do not leave stale cards. Changing the signed-in session clears results and drafts and discards obsolete responses.

Results are 20 per page with an HMAC cursor bound to principal, exact filter request, and 15-minute expiry. A search traversal is limited to 10 pages; privacy-filtered global totals are intentionally absent. The instance has a bounded per-principal 30/minute limiter with burst 10 and the existing overall request budget. A multi-instance production deployment still needs distributed enforcement. The current PostgreSQL query is suitable for this foundation and has not been benchmarked at global population scale.

## Eligibility is retired

Earlier versions required a trusted `adult` record from `discovery_eligibility` (migration `0005_discovery_eligibility.sql`) for the viewer and every candidate, and answered `403 DISCOVERY_ELIGIBILITY` when it was missing. That integration was never built, so no real account could search. Hiyall is for Gen Z and not adult-only: the gate is removed from people search and shared spaces, `store.EligibleAdult` and the error are gone, and every active, onboarded member is eligible. The table is kept in the schema for migration history, marked retired, and nothing reads or writes it. The minimum age is a self-declared 13 and is not age assurance; the consequences, and the follow-ups the owner may want (age-banded discovery, stricter defaults for under-18s), are in the [safety note](../api/19-nearby-and-open-access.md#safety-note-for-the-owner) and are not implemented.

**Nearby** is a separate list of people close to the member's coarse stored location (`POST /v1/discover/nearby`, which takes the same filters as this search plus a radius); it is described in [Nearby and open access](../api/19-nearby-and-open-access.md).

Age and gender filters exist in the service (`ageMin`, `ageMax`, `gender`; see [age bands](../api/19-nearby-and-open-access.md#age-bands)) but results carry no age or gender (a profile opened from a result shows whole years of age when its audience allows). No exact distance, last-seen status, verification badge, profile-media filter, connection-goal filter, mutual-connection explanation, premium filter, or preference persistence is shown because those capabilities lack suitable authoritative data or workflows. No gender is inferred from pronouns. No ages are guessed from the user's unrelated sample JSON.

## Discover and filter refinement

The current UI retains the original hero headline and adds a small static orbital mark, a compact lime action, and a bordered privacy row leading to the existing settings. The app describes discovery opt-in as a preference; the app's discovery and settings screens are being simplified separately from this server change. Internal account links remain behind the unchanged `devLoginEnabled` debug/release guard and are collapsed by default; release builds never render them.

Filters have the new subtitle, genuine owner-based quick shortcuts, a removable active-filter summary and total, and clear actions near the summary and in the sticky footer. All languages now clears only the language dimension; Search languages opens the existing searchable multi-selector. More ways to connect visibly advertises Location and Country and remains collapsed initially. Other dimensions are absent because the unchanged search service does not support them. The service has no total-count endpoint, so the action continues to say Find people rather than inventing a match count or treating the current page as a total.

Result cards present only public fields already returned by the API: name, handle, initial avatar, headline/bio, country, language names, up to four interests, and vibe. Match explanations retain their existing field-backed derivation. View profile uses the existing route; no unavailable friendship or message command is introduced. Static skeleton cards appear only during actual requests, and entry/removal/selection motion respects reduced-motion settings. Empty results use the quiet-orbit copy with explicit Clear filters and Explore everyone actions that run the existing search. Existing filter-widening actions remain available.

Connectivity failures say Couldn’t connect rather than claiming definite offline status: the existing ApiFailure contract cannot distinguish an offline device from an unreachable service. Authentication, validation, and rate-limit failures keep their actionable existing messages. Backend services, schemas, API contracts, authentication, search request/pagination methods, core models/state, navigation, and other screens were not modified by this refinement.

## Applied selections on Discover

Applied filters appear directly under the Discover title in a pinned header, above results. Each country, language, interest, and vibe has its own labeled, removable chip; country and language display human-readable names. A true selection count and Clear all sit above the chips. Removing one or clearing all runs the existing search with the remaining selections and resets pagination through its unchanged search method. These selections remain temporary and never write profile preferences.

The summary wraps across rows in both themes with 48-pixel minimum touch targets. For larger selections or enlarged text, the top summary scrolls with a visible scrollbar instead of collapsing values behind a +N count; its minimum height accommodates a complete chip. It stays pinned while the result list scrolls. Controls are disabled during an active request. No additional search dimensions or unsupported matching tabs were introduced from the reference screenshots. Widget tests verify pinned positions, preserved wire filters after removal, Clear all, and accessibility of the last long selection on a 320-pixel phone at doubled text size.

## Current simplified Discover layout

Discover opens directly on the existing filterable people list. A compact Find people heading and Explore Discover menu replace the introductory card and recommendation rails. Circles and Moments remain available through that menu with their existing data and actions. The standalone /people route remains supported. The pinned shortcut tabs (Everyone, Same vibe, Your interests, Your languages) use actual owner fields, followed by a horizontally scrolling row containing Filters, the applied count, removable outlined chips, and Clear all. Pull to refresh repeats the current search.

Each person is one row: a pastel initial avatar with a country-code tag, display name, up to three language codes with dots for the reported proficiency, a one-line headline, bio or vibe, and any field-backed match reasons. A muted chevron and the whole row open the existing profile route. Screen readers receive the complete public details. A compact visibility-settings link appears when discovery opt-in is off. No profile photos or online indicators are invented when the search response does not supply them.

Filters open in a bottom sheet. Country has full-width Anywhere and Choose country rows. Language has a searchable Choose languages row with selected names and an Any language reset; interests and vibe remain selectable chips. Reset and Show people stay in the footer. Draft cancellation, limits, wire values, request supersession, pagination, profile access checks, and privacy behavior retain their existing implementation. Age, gender and proximity are not added by this presentation change.

Focused widget checks cover the main Discover route, access to Circles and Moments, filter cancellation, applied-filter preservation and removal, loading and error states, and responsive layouts in both themes including 320-pixel phones with doubled text. Screenshot profiles are test fixtures only.

## Premium Discover layout ("Find your people.")

This supersedes the layouts above (the pinned tab row, the applied-filter chip row and the chevron rows are gone). The screen is in the same "Midnight Social Club" language as Home and Chats (`ChatTokens`, lime used sparingly, lavender for what people share, flat rows with hairlines, `PressScale` instead of ink splashes). Code: `lib/features/discovery.dart` (state and orchestration), `discover_header.dart` (`DiscoveryHeader`, `SurpriseCard`, `DiscoveryExploreRow`, `DiscoveryFilterButton`, `DiscoveryCategoryChips`), `discover_person.dart` (`DiscoverPersonTile`, `ContextLine`, `LanguageSummary`, `SayHiButton`), `discover_states.dart` (`DiscoveryEmptyState`, `DiscoveryErrorCard`, `DiscoveryLoadingState`), `discover_style.dart` (colours, checked for AA), `say_hi.dart`, `core/surprise.dart`. Previews: `.runtime/discover-previews-v3/` (`flutter test test/discover_capture_test.dart --dart-define=CAPTURE_DISCOVER=true`).

**Top to bottom (390 px phone, first three people fully visible, a fourth peeking above the dock):**

1. App header (unchanged), then **Find your people.** (29 px, capped at 1.3x text scale) with the compass as a small icon button (Circles, Moments).
2. **Surprise card**, about 88 px at 390 px (see the polish pass below): "Someone new is one hello away", "New faces. Unexpected conversations." and the **Surprise me →** button, with a small starburst on two orbits.
3. **Explore people**, with a compact **Filters** button on the right. The button shows a lime count of the filters that are on and, then, a **Reset**. Below 360 px or at large text it keeps only its icon (and its count); it is always a 48 dp target with the label "Filters, N active".
4. **Category chips**, pinned under the row while the list scrolls, with soft fades where they can scroll: **Everyone, Online, Same vibe, Interests, Languages, Nearby**. Same vibe, Interests and Languages come from the member's own profile and appear only when it has that field (as before). Online is the server filter `online: true`; Nearby is the existing Nearby route and stays last. A chip is selected when the current filters equal exactly what it stands for. The filter count and Reset only count filters no chip stands for, so choosing Online shows the Online chip and no badge, while Online plus a gender shows "2".
5. **People rows**, flat, hairline-divided, 72 to 96 px by content: avatar (photo or gradient, country badge, the shared `PresenceDot` top left only when the server said they are online), name, **one context line**, one line about them (headline, else bio, else vibe), a **languages line**, and the **Say hi** pill.

**Context line.** What the two share first ("2 in common · Music, Photography": `sharedInterests` from the server, in the viewer's own spelling), else their top three interests ("Music · Photography · Art"), else nothing. Nothing is made up when a field is missing.

**Languages line.** Names, never codes or dots: "Speaks English", "Speaks English, Hindi", "Speaks English, Hindi +1", and for one language with a stated level "English · Native". In Nearby the "Within 5 km" bucket leads this line. Rows use no fixed-direction pieces and ellipsize, so right-to-left and mixed scripts keep the layout.

**Say hi.** A compact pill (icon only under 360 px or at large text, still labelled "Say hi to Mira"). Tapping it, and only tapping it, calls the same `engine.open(userId)` the Message button on a profile uses and opens the conversation (the first-chat intro card with shared interests is already there). Viewing a row creates nothing. While it waits the pill shows a spinner and every other pill rests; on success it shows a check for a moment and then opens the chat, resting for 3 seconds in case the member returns; on a rate limit (429) the server's message and its `Retry-After` are shown in a snackbar and the pills rest for that long (at most a minute); other failures show the usual chat error. Chat runs only on Android and iOS: on the web the pill opens the profile, where the existing Message notice explains. Tapping the row itself opens the profile as before.

**Surprise me.** It opens a profile, never a chat, and is not random matching (no queue, no pairing, nothing is stored). It picks at random from the people already loaded for this viewer (the server has applied blocks, age bands, discoverability and suspensions), loading up to three more pages first when fewer than 10 are loaded. It never repeats the last pick and skips people the viewer already follows (first page of the following list, best effort) while others remain. States: ready, "Finding someone…", "No one new right now. Try again in a bit." and an error with Retry. The card only calls its `onSurprise` callback, so a future matching flow can replace what that does without touching the card; see `core/surprise.dart`. It picks from the current category or filters, as loaded.

**States.** Skeleton rows while loading (no spinner over the screen), "No one here just yet." with "Clear filters" when filters are on or Refresh when not, "Nobody’s online right now" with "Show everyone" for the Online chip, an error card with a real Retry ("Couldn’t connect." for a lost connection), the existing location explanation for Nearby without a position, a small loader and "Explore more people" for paging, pull to refresh. The first page of people eases in once; later pages and reduced motion do not animate.

**Filters sheet.** The existing sheet, plus an **Online now** switch (the same server filter as the chip), a lime "N on" count in the title and **Reset all**. Selections are kept while the member moves between docked screens and cleared when the account changes; they are never written to the profile. Interests and Vibe in the sheet remain the shared-interest style filters.

**Responsive and accessible.** Phone first; on tablets and the web the content is a centred column of at most 720 px. Rows read "Mira, 2 in common: Music, Photography, online" with the hint "Opens profile"; chips announce their selected state; touch targets are 48 dp. Both themes use only `ChatTokens` and `VibeColors`; every foreground/background pair is covered by an AA contrast test (`DiscoverStyle.pairs`).

**Dropped from the previous layout:** the removable applied-filter chip row (the count, Reset and the sheet replace it), the "Into X"/"Speaks Y" match reasons under rows (the context line replaces them) and the language-code dots.


## Polish pass: final row anatomy, Say hi, languages, country, long press

This supersedes the row, Say hi and languages paragraphs above. Previews: `.runtime/discover-previews-v3/` (`flutter test test/discover_capture_test.dart --dart-define=CAPTURE_DISCOVER=true`).

**Row anatomy (never more than two lines under the name).** Avatar (photo or gradient, 14 px presence dot with a surface-colour ring at the bottom right, only when the server said `online: true`), then the name with a small green "online" word beside it (11.5 px, capped at 1.3x text, `PresenceDot.textColor`, AA on both themes; the name ellipsizes first), then at most two secondary lines from `discoverRowLines`:

1. Context: what you share, in natural words from the real `sharedInterests`: one "You both love Travel", two "You both like Music and Travel", three or more "3 in common · Music, Travel". Otherwise their own top three interests ("Music · Photography · Art"). Casing is kept as given; there is no per-interest verb list.
2. The headline, else bio, else vibe, muted, one line.

In Nearby the distance bucket ("Within 5 km", mint) leads line 1; it never adds a third line.

**Languages line.** Not shown by default: full detail stays on the profile. It appears only (a) when the Languages category or any language filter is on, as "Speaks English, Hindi +1" (it then takes line 2, in place of the bio, so the two-line cap holds), or (b) when the row would otherwise have no context and no bio, so it is never empty. The spoken (screen reader) label still includes the languages, the online state, the distance and the bio.

**Say hi.** A small tonal control: 36 px to see, 44 dp to hit, the wave icon alone, named "Say hi to <name>" for screen readers and in its tooltip. Until the member has said hi once (a successful one; saved per account in the platform keystore, `core/say_hi_prefs.dart`, the same pattern as `home_prefs.dart`) it also shows the words "Say hi"; after the first success the words disappear everywhere. While the saved choice is still being read it is icon-only, so a returning member never sees the word flash. Under 360 px or at large text it is always icon-only. Pending (spinner), done (check), the 3 second rest, the rate-limit wait and the web fallback (opens the profile) are unchanged.

**First chat.** Say hi opens the conversation exactly as before (the first-chat intro card with the shared interests is what the member lands on). The conversation route now glides in (fade plus a 4 percent rise, 220 ms) from any entry point; with reduced motion it just appears. There is no Hero on the avatar: the list avatar and the conversation header are in different scroll and layout contexts, and a Hero there is costly and fragile. No new screens were added.

**Country badge.** Decision: rows do not show the country code ("IN", "KR"). It gives no context and can read as a precise location. It is one reversible switch, `DiscoverStyle.showCountryBadge` (default `false`; set it to `true` and the badge returns with the online dot moved to the top left). The country stays on the profile and in the filters; rows do not fall back to country text for people with nothing else to show. Distance buckets still appear only in the Nearby category.

**Long press / More options.** A long press on a row, or the "More options" custom action for TalkBack and VoiceOver, opens a compact sheet: View profile, Report, Block. Report uses the same `showReportSheet` as the profile and chat menus; Block asks first with `confirmBlock` and then calls the shared `blockAccount` helper in `safety.dart`. After a successful block (or a report that also blocks) the person leaves the list at once and the server's block exclusion applies to later loads. A failed block or report shows the error and keeps the row; "Account blocked." is never shown unless the server accepted it.

**Chips.** The selected chip is scrolled fully into view when it is selected (centred when there is room, 200 ms, instant for a restored selection and with reduced motion); only the chip row moves, never the page. The soft edge fade stays.

**Surprise card.** About 88 px at 390 px: the title, then the line and the 44 dp button side by side, with a small starburst beside the title. Below 360 px or above 1.1x text the line and the button stack. All four states (ready, finding, nothing, error) fit at 320 px and at 1.3x and 2.0x text. It still opens a profile from the loaded results and never starts a chat; there is no random matching.


## One-tap Say hi

This supersedes every earlier Say hi paragraph above (the pill that opened the conversation, the 3 second rest and the "First chat" landing). Previews: `.runtime/discover-previews-v4/` (`say-hi-states`, `say-hi-sending`, `say-hi-toast`, `say-hi-rate-limited`, `say-hi-setting-up` and `say-hi-first-time-sheet`, each in dark and light at 390x844 and 320x640; run `flutter test test/discover_capture_test.dart --dart-define=CAPTURE_DISCOVER=true`).

**One tap sends a 👋 and the member stays on Discover**, so they can greet several people in a row. The message is exactly the wave emoji; the conversation draws it as the large emoji-only message ([chat experience](14-chat-experience.md)). Nothing navigates, no sheet opens (after the first time), the list keeps its scroll position and only the tapped control changes: each row's control and its note follow their own slice of `sayHiControllerProvider`, so the rows themselves are not rebuilt. The send is the profile Message button's `engine.open(userId)` followed by the composer's `engine.send(conversationId, peerId, "👋")`, through `HiChat` (`features/say_hi.dart`); the engine encrypts for every device and hands the ciphertext to the relay. Without chat (the web) the control still opens the profile, where the Message notice explains. Sending needs no confirmation after the first time and is never triggered by a long press or by the row tap.

**Control states** (`SayHiState`, a 40 px round tonal control with a 48 dp touch target, named "Say hi to <name>" for screen readers and in its tooltip; the words "Say hi" still show on it until the first wave worked, except under 360 px or at large text):

| State | Looks | Says | Taps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| idle | wave | "Say hi to <name>" | send |
| pending | small spinner | "Sending hi to <name>" | none |
| sent | check (180 ms fade and scale, none with reduced motion), green tint | "Hi sent to <name>" | none, for 24 hours |
| conversation exists | chat bubble | "Message <name>" | open that conversation |
| not accepting messages | grey wave and the line "<name> isn’t accepting messages from you." under the row | "Say hi to <name>" (disabled) | none for the session |
| rest (rate limit or daily quota) | all controls grey, the server's message with its wait under the row | disabled | none, at most a minute |
| failed | refresh arrow and the calm error under the row | "Try again: say hi to <name>" | send again |
| another wave is on its way | grey wave | disabled | none |

At most one wave is in flight: a second tap, a double tap or a tap on another row while one is on its way does nothing (the others are disabled), and a sent control cannot be tapped again. The wave is not marked sent after a failure.

**Sent for 24 hours.** After a wave worked the person stays "sent" for 24 hours, kept per account and per device in the platform keystore with the time it was sent (`SayHiPrefsStore.sent`, JSON `{peerId: UTC milliseconds}`, the same pattern as `say_hi_used`); it survives restarts, never leaks to another account on the phone and goes back to idle after 24 hours (the clock is injectable, `sayHiClockProvider`).

**Existing conversation.** If the member already has a conversation with the person that holds a message, the control is Message and opens it (no wave is sent). It is read from the chat list the Chats screen last loaded (`chatPeersProvider`, peer id to conversation id, only chats whose last message is known) plus the people the member just waved at; Discover makes no request of its own to find out, so before the chat list has been loaded once in the session nobody counts as a conversation, and the server's idempotent open means a wave to an existing chat is still safe.

**Messages.** 403 `MESSAGES_NOT_ALLOWED`: "<name> isn’t accepting messages from you." under the row and the control is disabled for the session. 429 `RATE_LIMITED` or `QUOTA`: the server's message with its `Retry-After` ("You’ve reached today’s limit for starting new chats. Try again in about 57 minutes."), every control rests for that long capped at a minute, nothing is marked sent. While the engine is still creating its keys (first use on the device) the row says "Setting up encryption…" with the spinner. Any other failure is calm ("Couldn’t reach the chat service. Check your connection.") and the same control becomes a retry. The notes are spoken when they appear.

**Feedback without leaving.** A short toast at the bottom of the list, "👋 sent to Mira", for 2 seconds. It is a live region (announced), takes no taps, is replaced (never stacked) by the next one and cannot be undone, because a sent message cannot be unsent. A light haptic on success. The toast and a rest end with the screen (the screen owns their timers).

**The first wave asks once.** Because one tap is irreversible, the member's first ever Say hi shows a small bottom sheet: "Send a 👋 to Mira?", "They’ll get it as a message. You can’t unsend it.", **Send hi** and Cancel. The choice is remembered per account (`SayHiPrefsStore.confirmed`) and the sheet never returns; cancelling sends nothing and remembers nothing. There is no "don't ask again" switch: asking once is the whole safeguard.

**Server limits.** One tap makes mass greeting easy, so the API caps how many conversations a member can *start* per rolling 24 hours (`Entitlements.NewConversationsPerDay`, 40 on Free; 429 `QUOTA` with a `Retry-After`), on top of the burst limit for new contacts; see [Nearby and open access](../api/19-nearby-and-open-access.md#daily-limit-on-starting-conversations). Reopening a conversation never counts. Nothing in the app mentions plans.

**Accessibility and motion.** Targets are 48 dp, text is capped at 1.3x inside the control and the notes wrap at 2.0x text at 320 px; every state pair (sent, disabled, note, toast) is covered by the AA test (`DiscoverStyle.pairs`); with reduced motion the check, the note and the toast just appear.


## Quiet rows and an icon-only wave

This supersedes the earlier paragraphs on the Say hi word, its shape and the row's press feedback. Previews: `.runtime/discover-previews-v5/` (`row-hovered-*`, `say-hi-states-*`, `say-hi-rate-limited-*`, `profile-wave-*` and `profile-wave-sent-*`, dark and light at 390x844 and 320x640; run `flutter test test/discover_capture_test.dart --dart-define=CAPTURE_DISCOVER=true`).

- **No grey block on a row.** The row's `PressScale` was given `highlight: text at 7%`, a deliberate tint drawn behind the whole tappable area (avatar to the Say hi button, offset from the divider) that showed at half strength on mouse hover and fully while pressed. Nearby is the same list and rows, so it is fixed too. The Chats list still passes `highlight` (not part of this pass), and the Followers/Following rows use a plain `InkWell`.

Sent is a check in the accent colour (lime on dark, deep green on light). The 48 dp target, the semantics labels, tooltips, toast, first-time confirmation sheet, haptics, rest and reduced motion are unchanged.
- **Profile.** Another member's profile shows the same wave control right after Follow and Message (`SayHiProfileButton`, same `SayHiController`, states, confirmation and tap handling as a row, through `sayHiTap`). The sent toast shows as a snackbar there, and the note (not accepting messages, a rest, a failure) shows under the buttons. Hidden on one's own profile and on the web (no chat), where the Message notice explains as before.


## Wave 7: language levels, tags, filters, possible matches

Server rules and the API are in [Profile and discovery v2](../api/25-profile-and-discovery-v2.md). Previews: `.runtime/discover-previews-v7/`.

- **Rows.** Under the usual lines a row can show the member's local time ("3:42 PM there", only when they show it) and one line of chips that scrolls sideways: up to three languages (name and a six-bar level meter, read as "English, fluent") then up to two personality tags. Every chip keeps a 48 dp hit area and is the tag drill-down. A Nearby row of a member who hides their distance shows none.
- **Tag drill-down.** Tapping an interest, a personality tag or a language anywhere (a profile, a row) puts that filter on Discover and goes there (`openDiscoverWith`; Discover listens to `discoverFiltersProvider`, so it also reacts when already open). The active filters show as removable chips under the categories ("English, remove filter").
- **Filters sheet.** Languages (multi-select) with a minimum-level selector that appears once a language is chosen (any, Conversational, Fluent, Advanced, Native), a "Same country" shortcut switch (off, with the reason, when the member has no country; a server `COUNTRY_NOT_SET` says it and drops the switch), personality tags (any-of), interests, and Reset. Age and gender stay hidden for members under 18.
- **Saved filters.** The filters last used are kept on this device per account (`DiscoverPrefsStore`, in the platform keystore beside the other small choices), come back when Discover opens, and are removed at sign-out. A chip drill-down wins for that visit. Nearby and Possible matches are modes and are not saved. Nothing is sent to the server. The list loads at once; if saved filters turn up it loads again.
- **Possible matches.** A chip in the category row selects the mode (it clears other filters; picking another category leaves it). The list is the server's ranking with one line of why ("Shares your interest in Music and Travel · Similar personality", "Good language match", "Same country"). Empty state: add interests, languages and tags, with a button to edit the profile. A first-use tip (`discover.possible_matches`) explains it once.
