# Chat experience

The chat list and the conversation ("Midnight Social Club") are presentation only: `apps/mobile/lib/features/chat.dart` (screens and providers), `chat_list.dart`, `chat_header.dart`, `chat_thread.dart`, `chat_composer.dart`, `chat_intro.dart`, `chat_starters.dart`, `chat_menu.dart`, `chat_parts.dart`, `chat_format.dart`, `chat_photo.dart` and `chat_photo_states.dart`, with design tokens in `lib/shared/chat_tokens.dart` and the avatar gradient in `lib/shared/avatar_gradient.dart`. Encryption, the engine's send and receive, the outbox and the media cache are unchanged; the API gained one read-only field, `sharedInterests` (see New chat). Reactions and replies (below) are the exception: they add payload kinds to the engine and one hint to the send request. Encrypted chat runs on Android and iOS only; elsewhere both routes explain that and nothing else renders.

## Design tokens

`ChatTokens` is a `ThemeExtension` registered on both themes in `hiyallTheme` (and resolved by brightness under a plain `ThemeData`). Dark: page `#171B17`, raised `#1D221D`, surface `#242A24`, elevated `#2D342D`, hairline `#394238`, text `#F5F6EF` / `#ADB5A8` / `#949F91`, lime `#D2F65A`, softened outgoing lime `#C1EE8D` to `#B7E67C` (two close tones of one hue), lavender `#DCCBFF`. Light: warm paper `#F7F8F2`, white surfaces, ink text, deep green accent for text that is lime in dark. The light incoming bubble is a cooler, greyer `#E4E9E8` with a `#D3D9D6` hairline (the old white was only 1.07:1 from the page; now 1.15:1 and 0.13 darker in luminance, with ink on it at 13:1); `chat_tokens_test.dart` asserts a minimum luminance step in both themes, AA text on the new fill, and that the saturated lime stays distinct from the grey. The dark scaffold colour moved from `#191C18` to `#171B17` so chat and shell have no seam. Every text/background pair is asserted at WCAG AA in `test/chat_tokens_test.dart`. Sizes live in `ChatMetrics` (4/8 px system).

## Chat list

- **Heading:** logo row (the app shell), "Chats" (30 px) and a real subtitle computed from the conversations: "3 unread chats", "1 unread chat", "All caught up", "No conversations yet". Wide windows already print the title in the shell, so only the subtitle shows.
- **Search:** 52 px elevated pill, no visible edge until focused, then a soft lime ring. Placeholder "Search conversations", a clear icon when there is text, filtering 200 ms after the last keystroke (clearing is immediate). Matches name, username (with or without `@`) and last message.
- **Filters:** `All` and `Unread · n`, the only two backed by data. They combine with search. There is no "Active" filter: presence is only what each person lets you see.
- **Meet someone new:** a compact lime-tinted card under the filters ("Find people who share your vibe", "Start exploring →") that opens `/discover`. It matches nobody; it is hidden while searching or filtering.
- **Rows:** flat, 80 px, 52 px avatar, no card or border; a hairline starts under the text. Unread rows get a slightly brighter fill, a bolder name, a brighter preview, a lime time and a lime counter ("99+" above 99). Muted chats show a bell and a neutral counter. Press scales to 0.98 with a surface highlight (and a softer one on hover). Preview text comes from `previewFor`: text, "You: ", photo (or its caption), "Not sent" for a failed message, "Message" as the safe fallback, `@username` before any message; a typing peer shows "typing…".
- **Avatars:** photo, otherwise a gradient from a stable FNV-1a hash of the user id (`avatarGradient`), the same on every screen and run, with a dark readable initial. `PersonAvatar` uses it everywhere unless a caller passes a flat `tint` (notifications, Home cards).
- **Empty / no match / no unread / loading / error:** "No conversations yet" with the smiley art and **Meet someone new**; "No conversations found" (Clear search); "No unread conversations" (Show all chats); flat skeleton rows; "Setting up encryption…" and error cards with Try again.
- **Online dot:** a green `PresenceDot` (14 px, ring cut out in the row's own surface colour) at the bottom right of the avatar when the conversation payload says the peer is online and lets you see it (`peer.online`; key `chat-row-online-<userId>`, semantic label "online", and the row's spoken label includes "online"). Nothing is drawn otherwise: hidden and offline are the same to the app. It comes with the conversation list the app already loads (no profile fetch, so no visit is recorded), and the list reloads on the same signals as before plus a slow 60 s refresh while the realtime socket is live (without a socket the existing 30 s sync does it); pull to refresh also updates it. Shared interests appear only in the new-chat card, from the server field above, not in list rows.

## Navigation

The five tabs, routes and labels are unchanged ("Discover" is accurate). The dock is a floating rounded surface with a lime selected pill, 24 px icons, 11 px labels and an 18 px unread badge ("99+") at the icon's corner, above the safe area. **An open conversation (`/chats/:id`) hides the dock and the app header** (`AppShell.inConversation`); system back and the header back button return to the list, where the dock is back. The shell then leaves the bottom inset to the composer so the backdrop is unbroken.

## Conversation

- **Header** (56 px, never changes): back, avatar (40 px, 34 px under 360 px wide), the name at 20 px bold on one line with an ellipsis, one reserved 18 px subtitle line (see below), a mute mark, the safety shield and the menu. The shield is a visible 48 dp control (a tonal circle with a filled accent shield), labelled and with the tooltip "Safety and encryption"; it opens the safety number dialog, which now starts with a line explaining the encryption. Header text is clamped to 1.3x so nothing overflows at 320 px with system text at 2.0x. The subtitle line is chosen in this order: **typing…** (accent), **online** (green, with the green `PresenceDot` on the avatar), **last seen …**, then "end-to-end encrypted". Presence is the peer's `online` / `lastSeenAt` from `GET /v1/conversations/{id}`, read with the receipt and so refreshed on the same chat signals and the slow 60 s refresh; when the server omits both (the peer hides it, or was never seen) the line falls back to the encryption text, so a hidden peer never looks different from an offline one. Last-seen wording (`lastSeenText`, viewer's local time, injectable clock `chatClockProvider`, 12 or 24 hour as the device formats times): under 5 minutes "last seen just now", under an hour "last seen 25 min ago" (steps of five, because the server value is a 5-minute bucket and the text never claims more), later today "last seen today at 14:20", "last seen yesterday at 21:05", within a week "last seen Mon", older "last seen 12 Oct". Calendar days are compared by date, so 23:55 seen at 00:05 is "10 min ago" and from 01:00 "yesterday". The last-seen line scales down a little on the narrowest phones rather than being cut off. The reserved line keeps the header at 56 px in every variant (tested at 320 px and text 2.0x).
- **Backdrop:** page colour with two near-invisible ambient highlights (lavender, lime). No dots, no noise, no animation.
- **Bubbles:** 22 px outer radii; inside a run the joining corners are 6 px and the last bubble's tail corner 4 px (`bubbleRadii`, a pure function of position in the run, both sides; a bubble shorter than 44 px is scaled by the framework into a pill, so "ok" and "yes" are pills, not boxes). 15/10/15/9 px padding, 2 px between bubbles of a run and 11 px between runs. Outgoing is the softened lime with ink text; incoming a cooler grey surface with a hairline (light) or a raised surface (dark). Time and status sit in the last bubble of a run, measured in the theme's font so they never overlap the text. Emoji-only messages stay large and bubble-less. Long unbroken text wraps.
- **Photos:** a received photo is the blurred preview of the real picture with one of four states on a small dark plate (so the words read over any photo, and it scales down inside a 198 px wide photo): **loading** (a progress ring and "Loading photo…"), **failed** ("Couldn’t load photo" and a 44 dp Retry), **expired** ("Photo expired", "Photos are removed 30 days after they’re sent.", no button) and **damaged** ("Can’t open this photo"). An outgoing photo keeps its upload ring with Cancel, and a failed send keeps Retry and Delete under the bubble. No decorative placeholder art is ever drawn over a photo; the gradients in the screenshots are fake image data made by the preview test.
- **Motion** (all off with reduced motion, nothing continuous except the typing wave, nothing delays sending): a new bubble animates in over 200 ms (yours slides up 18 px from the composer while fading in, theirs settle 6 px); the send button dips to 0.88 under the thumb; the delivery mark cross-fades (160 ms) from clock to tick to double tick; the typing indicator is three dots in a staggered half-sine wave (560 ms each, 150 ms apart) followed by a 600 ms rest per 1.6 s breath, never a flash; the unread badge pops in as before.
- **Composer:** one elevated 56 px dock: attach (left), growing field (up to six lines, "Message…"), send (right, lime when there is text, never for blank text). It rides above the keyboard.
- **Scroll:** the thread follows new messages only when the person is already within about 160 px of the bottom, on first load, or right after they send (`shouldAutoScroll`). Otherwise a floating "scroll to latest" button appears with a count of messages that arrived meanwhile.
- **New chat:** a small card (about 300 px at normal text size), dismissible, never blocking the composer, gone once there is a message. It says "Say hi to Niko 👋", then:
  - **A shared-interest line when the server found one** ("You both love Music 🎧", "You both like Gaming 🎮", for two "You both like Music 🎧 and Gaming 🎮"; a custom interest as typed). The data is `sharedInterests` on every conversation payload (list, get, open): up to three interests both members list, matched case-insensitively and spelled and ordered as the caller wrote them, `[]` when nothing is shared. **The server decides**: the peer's interests are compared only when the peer's profile audience and `interests` field audience admit the caller (everyone, followers, friends, nobody, as on the profile), blocked and age-barred pairs never see the conversation at all, and nothing is recorded as a profile visit (see `docs/api/19-nearby-and-open-access.md`). The app only reflects it (`parseSharedInterests` in the engine model: missing, null or malformed is empty). The card waits for the first conversation read so its chips do not change under the thumb. Nothing is invented or inferred on the device; there is no match claim.
  - **Three or four icebreaker chips** (`chat_starters.dart`): with shared interests, one or two about the first interest, one about the second (or another about the first), and one easy general opener; with none, four general ones. A client-side table covers the twelve onboarding interests (Music, Technology, Travel, Photography, Movies, Art, Gaming, Reading, Fitness, Food, Nature, Learning), four short openers each; anything else falls back to the general pool. The set is rotated by a stable hash of the conversation id, so the same chat always shows the same chips. Tone is casual and curious: no questions about age, school, location, appearance, contact details or photos, nothing flirty (a test scans every starter). **A chip only fills the composer** for the person to edit; it never sends.
  - **A safety line**: "You’re in control: report or block anytime" with small **Report** and **Block** text buttons (44 dp targets). They call the same handlers as the conversation menu (`reportPeer`, `blockPeer`): the report sheet, and the block confirmation (`confirmBlock`, shared with the profile menu) followed by the block call and leaving the chat.
  - Dismissing keeps only the quiet encryption chip above the empty thread (the empty-conversation state).
- **Menu:** mute / unmute (with duration), view profile, report (its sheet also offers block) and block (confirmation first, then it closes the chat). Nothing reports success unless the action succeeded. There is no "end chat" concept in the product, and no random matching or "next match": that is a separate product decision.
- **Reactions and replies:** see [Reactions and replies](#reactions-and-replies). Long-press now opens the message menu.
- **Not done:** link detection, video and voice messages, **editing and unsending (deleting for the peer) messages**, a delivered tick (the relay sends only sent and read), and a two-pane layout on wide windows.

## Reactions and replies

Code: `lib/features/chat_reactions_ui.dart` (menu, chips, quote, reply bar, notice and the gestures), `chat_thread.dart` (the bubble), `chat_composer.dart` (the `above` slot) and `chat.dart` (state). The protocol, validation and ordering are in [the security record](../security/13-encrypted-chat-implementation.md#reactions-and-replies). Everything sits behind the existing chat capability checks, so none of it exists on the web (where chat is unavailable).

- **Menu.** Long press (touch) or right click (desktop) on a delivered message opens a floating menu: the six quick reactions in a 56 dp pill (❤️ 😂 👍 😮 😢 🔥, each a 48 dp target), then **Reply** and **Copy** (text only, not on photos). Everything except the message dims, and the message stays bright in a hole cut in the dim, so the pill reads as belonging to it. A message half under the header or composer is first scrolled fully into view. The pill sits above the message when there is room and below it otherwise, aligned to the message's own side and kept 8 dp from the window edges (it is 298 dp wide, so it fits 320 dp). The reaction already set is ringed, and picking it again clears it. A failed, sending or notice row has no menu. Motion: the menu fades and scales in 170 ms; with reduced motion it is simply there.
- **Double tap** on a text bubble toggles ❤️ (a photo's tap opens the viewer, so photos use the menu). The pop is a heart that grows past its size and fades in about half a second; no pop with reduced motion.
- **Chips.** Reactions sit under the bubble, aligned to its side, never over the bubble or its time, never wider than the bubble's maximum width (they wrap). A chip is the emoji, plus a count **only when both people chose the same emoji** (so the only count is 2). Yours is ringed and tinted in the accent; the other person's is a plain surface chip. Tapping your own chip removes your reaction; theirs does nothing. Each chip is a 48 dp target; the visible chip is 32 dp. A new chip settles in with a small overshoot (none with reduced motion). The chat list preview and unread counts are not affected, and a reaction never creates a bubble.
- **Reply.** Swipe a bubble to the right (it follows the finger a short way, 30 dp, half that on your own bubbles which sit against the edge, and a reply icon appears; past 56 dp a light haptic fires and releasing starts the reply) or choose Reply. A **"Replying to Mira" bar** appears above the composer: an accent bar, who, one line of the quoted message (Photo, with a camera icon, for an image) and a 48 dp close button. The thread keeps the newest message in view while the bar grows. Sending (text, or a photo) carries the quote and closes the bar; a failed send keeps it; closing it keeps whatever is typed.
- **Quote in the bubble.** The reply bubble starts with a compact block: an accent bar, the author ("You" or the peer's name, bold) and one line of the quoted text, ellipsised; the bubble is as wide as the wider of the quote and the text and the quote fills it. When the original is in local history **its** author and text are shown, not what the sender embedded (the quote is attributed text, not proof). A quoted photo shows "Photo". Tapping the quote scrolls to the original and lights it with a soft wash for 1.4 seconds; if the original is not loaded, nothing happens and nothing is shown. A reply to a reply quotes only the reply's own text.
- **Failure.** A reaction that cannot be sent is taken back and a small notice appears above the composer ("Couldn't send your reaction. Check your connection." with **Retry** when the failure is transient). It clears itself after six seconds or on tap. It never blocks anything.
- **Accessibility.** Each message offers the custom actions **Reply**, **React with ❤️** and **Choose a reaction**. The menu is a labelled route; pill entries read "React with 😂" or "Remove ❤️ reaction". Chips read "You reacted with ❤️", "Mira reacted with 😂" or "You and Mira reacted with ❤️" (your own is a button, "Double tap to remove your reaction"). The quote reads "Replying to You: …" and the message "…, replying to You: …: text". The reply bar reads "Replying to Mira: …". Targets are 48 dp; the new chrome keeps a 1.3x text-scale clamp, the emoji and bubbles follow the system scale. Tested at 320 dp wide with text at 2.0x in both themes. Contrast pairs (own chip, menu, reply bar, notice, quote name and line in both bubbles, light and dark) are asserted at WCAG AA in `test/chat_reactions_ui_test.dart`.

Preview captures for these are `.runtime/chat-previews-v6/` (`bubbles-with-chips-*`, `reaction-menu-*`, `reaction-menu-own-*`, `reply-bar-*`, `reply-bar-typed-*`, `reply-bar-photo-*`, `reaction-failed-notice-*`, `swipe-to-reply-*`), in dark and light at 390x844 and 320x640 and at 320x640 with text 2.0, from `chat_preview_test.dart` with `CAPTURE_CHAT=true --plain-name "reactions:"`. The reaction emoji are drawn with the Mac's own colour emoji font, which the preview test loads when present; without it they are boxes, as elsewhere in the previews.

## Emoji and the square boxes in screenshots

The boxes in earlier screenshots are the test runner's fonts: `flutter test` ships no emoji font, so any emoji draws as a missing glyph. The app does not strip or replace emoji anywhere: nothing in the send path, the message model, the stored history, the list preview or the report evidence touches the text beyond `trim()`, and the large rendering draws the exact string. `test/chat_emoji_test.dart` pins this for ZWJ sequences (family, couple), skin tones, flags (including a subdivision flag), keycaps and hearts through `messageFromBody`, the message JSON, `ChatHistory` over a fresh store, the previews, `isEmojiOnly` and the large rendering (sized 52, 44 or 38 by grapheme count, not code points). Checking it found two gaps in `isEmojiOnly`, both fixed: keycaps (1️⃣) and subdivision flags were not recognised as emoji, so they rendered as normal bubbles instead of large.

## A wave from Discover

The one-tap Say hi on Discover ([people discovery](12-people-discovery.md#one-tap-say-hi)) sends the single character 👋 as an ordinary text message through the engine's normal send path. A message that is only that emoji is not special on the wire; in the thread it is an emoji-only message (`isEmojiOnly`: one to three emoji and nothing else), so it renders large and bubble-less (52 px for a single emoji), on both sides, with the usual time and delivery mark, and the chat list preview and the unread count treat it like any other text. The recipient sees it arrive as a normal message (push, badge, list row "👋"); nothing about it says it came from Discover. The conversation's first-chat card ("Say hi to <name> 👋") is unchanged for chats started elsewhere.
